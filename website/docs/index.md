## Why Senrigan?

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-laptop:{ .lg .middle } __Offline & self-contained__

    ---

    Runs entirely on your laptop with a single `make up` — **no SIEM, no cloud infrastructure**.

-   :material-target:{ .lg .middle } __100+ built-in hunts__

    ---

    Ready-to-run AWS CloudTrail threat hunts covering compromise, privilege escalation and exfiltration.

-   :material-robot:{ .lg .middle } __AI chat analysis__

    ---

    Investigate your CloudTrail data in natural language with AI-assisted analysis.

-   :material-chart-box:{ .lg .middle } __80+ dashboard charts__

    ---

    Pre-built Apache **Superset** BI dashboards to visualize activity at a glance.

-   :material-file-document:{ .lg .middle } __Reports & Suzaku__

    ---

    Generate HTML threat-hunting reports and visualize [Suzaku](https://github.com/Yamato-Security/suzaku) results.

-   :material-graph:{ .lg .middle } __AWS Config resource graph__

    ---

    Explore your account's resources and their relationships as a graph.

</div>
